> **Note on honesty.** Extraction metrics in this report come from a **seeded simulated LLM**: the ground truth of each synthetic document with realistic errors injected at fixed rates (see *Error model*). ## Truncation recovery

14 of 225 first answers were cut at the output limit. One retry in summary mode recovered **7**; 7 went to human review. Retries: 14 calls, $0.1132.

A naive loop that repeats the identical request up to 3 times would have made 42 extra full-budget calls and, at temperature 0, recovered none of them (the answer is cut at the same place every time). **Cost avoided: $0.3726** on this corpus.
