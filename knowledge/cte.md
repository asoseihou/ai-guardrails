# CT-e: electronic transport document

## What it is
The CT-e (Conhecimento de Transporte Eletrônico, model 57) documents a cargo transport service. It is issued by the carrier, and its auxiliary printout is called DACTE. Like the NF-e it has a 44-digit access key, with model 57 in positions 21-22.

## Parties
A CT-e names several parties, and confusing them is a common extraction error:
- Emitente: the carrier that issues the CT-e.
- Remetente: the shipper, who sends the cargo.
- Destinatário: the consignee, who receives the cargo.
- Expedidor and recebedor: optional intermediate parties that hand over or receive the cargo.
- Tomador do serviço: who pays for the transport; it is often the shipper or the consignee and is printed in its own box.

## Value of the service
The DACTE lists the components of the service value (componentes do valor da prestação), such as FRETE PESO (weight-based freight), AD VALOREM (a percentage of the cargo value), GRIS (cargo risk management fee), PEDÁGIO (tolls) and collection or delivery fees. The total value of the service (valor total da prestação) is the sum of the components. The cargo value (valor da carga) is a different number: it is the value of the goods, not of the transport.

## Referenced documents
The CT-e lists the documents of the cargo it carries, usually the access keys of the NF-e that accompany the goods (documentos originários). Those keys have model 55, so an extractor must not confuse them with the CT-e's own key.

## Modal and ICMS
The CT-e records the transport mode (rodoviário for road, aéreo, aquaviário, ferroviário, dutoviário or multimodal). Interstate and intermunicipal transport services are subject to ICMS, which is why the CT-e carries a CFOP such as 5353 or 6353.
